# Playlists and ratings

## Playlists

Your playlists are listed under **Playlists** in the sidebar.

- **New playlist** in the sidebar creates an empty one.
- **Add to playlist** in a track's right-click menu adds tracks to an existing playlist or to a new one.
- Open a playlist to play it. In a playlist, **Remove from playlist** (or **Delete**) takes tracks out of it.

Playlists remember tracks, not file paths: when you move or rename files in your library folders, they stay in your playlists. A track whose file is gone stays in the playlist, shown as missing, and comes back when the file returns.

## Ratings

Give a track one to five stars with **Rate** in its right-click menu, or choose **Not rated** to clear it. Ratings are kept in Helikodu's library and survive moves and renames of the file.

When Helikodu first reads a file, it takes over a rating that another player wrote into the tags (for example with MusicBee, Quod Libet, foobar2000 or Picard).

Writing ratings and other changes into the files' tags comes in a later version. **Edit tags…** is shown in the menus already and says so.
