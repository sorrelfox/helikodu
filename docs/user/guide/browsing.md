# Browsing albums

The main window has three parts: the **sidebar** on the left, the **content** in the middle and the **panel** on the right, which shows the queue. Drag the borders between them to change their widths. **F9** hides or shows the sidebar, and **F6** moves the keyboard focus from one part to the next.

## The Albums view

**Albums** shows your albums as a grid of covers. Use **Sort by** to order them by title, album artist, year or date added. Albums whose tracks have no album tag are grouped under **Unknown album**; compilations are listed under **Various artists**.

Covers come from pictures embedded in the files and from image files in the album folder, such as `cover.jpg`, `folder.png` or `front.webp`. Albums without a cover get a generated placeholder.

The other views in the sidebar (for example **Artists**, **Genres** and **Tracks**) say when they arrive in a later version. Search is not available yet either.

## An album page

Double-click an album cover to play the album at once. A single click opens its page: the cover, the album's details (release date, genre, size, folder) and its tracks, grouped by disc. **Alt+Left** and **Alt+Right** go back and forward between the pages you visited.

On the album page, **Play** plays the album from the start and **Shuffle** in random order. Click a track to play the album from that track.

## The track menu

Right-click a track for its menu (an album cover's menu has **Play** and **Go to album**):

| Entry | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Play** | Plays it now |
| **Play next** | Puts it in the queue after the playing track |
| **Add to queue** | Adds it to the end of the queue |
| **Add to playlist** | Adds it to a playlist, or to a new one |
| **Go to album**, **Go to artist** | Opens that page |
| **Rate** | Gives it one to five stars |
| **Open containing folder** | Shows the file in your file manager |
